DOCUMENT: COM-PROC-001
TITLE: Communication Subsystem Recovery Procedure
SUBSYSTEM: Communication
VERSION: 2.8

1. OVERVIEW
This document describes recovery procedures for communication link degradation.

2. NORMAL OPERATING RANGES
- Signal Strength: -60 dBm to -85 dBm (nominal: -75 dBm)
- Packet Loss: 0% - 2% (nominal: <0.5%)
- Communication Latency: 100ms - 400ms (nominal: 250ms)

3. DEGRADATION INDICATORS
a) Signal strength below -90 dBm
b) Packet loss exceeding 5%
c) Latency exceeding 800ms
d) Frequent link drops

4. DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE
Step 1: Verify antenna pointing accuracy
Step 2: Check ground station availability
Step 3: Test secondary communication channel
Step 4: Review interference patterns
Step 5: Assess transponder health

5. RECOVERY ACTIONS (HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED)
Action 1: Switch to backup transponder
Action 2: Increase transmission power
Action 3: Adjust antenna orientation
Action 4: Contact alternate ground station

WARNING: All communication actions require human operator approval.
