[SIMULATED HISTORICAL INCIDENT LOG — FOR DEMONSTRATION PURPOSES ONLY]

INCIDENT: HIST-0021
TITLE: Battery Cell Degradation Event — Mission Day 127
SEVERITY: HIGH
STATUS: RESOLVED
SUBSYSTEM: Power

SUMMARY:
On Mission Day 127, progressive battery voltage decline was detected over a 4-hour period. Root cause analysis identified degradation in battery cell group B, resulting in reduced capacity.

OBSERVED PARAMETERS:
- Battery voltage decreased from 28.1V to 25.3V over 4 hours
- Battery current increased from 5.1A to 7.8A compensating for voltage loss
- Battery temperature elevated to 38°C during peak charging
- Solar charging input remained normal at 2.0 kW

ROOT CAUSE:
Battery cell group B showed increased internal resistance consistent with aging/degradation. Cycling count had exceeded 1,500 cycles with operating in higher temperature ranges.

RESOLUTION:
- Battery conditioning cycle performed
- Load shedding of non-essential payload
- Enhanced monitoring enabled
- Capacity reduced from 100% to 82% of original

LESSONS LEARNED:
1. Battery temperature monitoring should trigger earlier alerts
2. Current increase is an early indicator of voltage degradation
3. Historical trending analysis recommended for proactive detection
