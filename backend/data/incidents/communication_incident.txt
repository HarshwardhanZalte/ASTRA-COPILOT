INCIDENT: HIST-0015
TITLE: S-Band Link Degradation — Mission Day 74
SEVERITY: MEDIUM
STATUS: RESOLVED
SUBSYSTEM: Communication

SUMMARY:
Mission Day 74: Gradual S-band link degradation observed during southern hemisphere passes.

OBSERVED PARAMETERS:
- Signal strength degraded from -74 dBm to -91 dBm
- Packet loss increased from 0.3% to 8.7%
- Communication latency 620ms (nominal: 250ms)

ROOT CAUSE:
Rain fade at primary ground station combined with non-optimal antenna pointing due to attitude control drift.

RESOLUTION:
- Switched to secondary ground station
- Re-established link at -78 dBm
- Attitude correction maneuver scheduled

LESSONS LEARNED:
1. Multiple ground stations required for continuous coverage
2. Attitude drift can cause gradual signal degradation
